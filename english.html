Nouns and Verbs

Nouns name things. Verbs are actions or states.

Adjectives and Adverbs

Adjectives describe nouns. Adverbs often end in -ly and describe how.

Spelling

Use common patterns. Some words are irregular and must be memorised.

Homophones

They sound the same but mean different things: there/their/they're; to/too/two.

Punctuation

Questions end with ?. Use ! for strong feeling and , to separate ideas.

Conjunctions and Possession

Conjunctions join parts (and, because). Use apostrophe+s for singular possession.
