Maths Class

Learn the ideas, then start the questions.

Addition

Add numbers by lining up ones, tens and hundreds.

Subtraction

Subtract larger minus smaller. Borrow from the next place value if needed.

Times Tables

Use 2–12 times tables. Practice 3,4,8 then 6,7,9,11 and 12.

Fractions of amounts

Find a fraction of a number: divide by the denominator, then multiply by the numerator.

Perimeter

Perimeter of a rectangle is 2 × (length + width).

Rounding

Nearest 10: last digit 5–9 rounds up, 0–4 down. Nearest 100: look at tens digit.
